01 / THE CHALLENGE

Spot a problem.
Build a response.

Ideathon is a two-round student innovation challenge presented by Aavishkar 27.

Start online with a PPT explaining your idea in one of the listed domains. Teams shortlisted from Round 1 move on to a 30-hour, in-person implementation round at Government College of Engineering, Karad.

Round 1 is free. Teams selected for implementation pay ₹400 per team. Bring your own equipment for building a working model.

Who can participate?+

Teams must have at least 2 and no more than 4 students. The event rulebook specifies one submission per team.

Is there an entry fee?+

Round 1 idea submission is free. Shortlisted teams pay ₹400 per team to participate in the implementation round.

What happens in Round 2?+

Round 2 is a 30-hour offline implementation round at GCE, Karad. Teams build a working model and bring their own equipment.

Which domains can we choose?+

Construction, agriculture, electro-mechanical, robotics, IT, renewable energy, defence, rural development and space.

When are the event dates?+

Dates will be announced soon. Check the updates section for registration and submission announcements.

What are the prizes?+

Winner: ₹7,000 · 1st Runner Up: ₹5,000 · 2nd Runner Up: ₹3,000.
